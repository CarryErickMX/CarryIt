CarryIt

Download videos, or just the audio, from YouTube and other sites, using yt-dlp and FFmpeg.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

Only download content you own or that its author allows you to download (for example, your own videos or freely licensed content).

Downloading YouTube videos may violate YouTube's Terms of Service. You are responsible for how you use this program.

The program is available in English and Spanish; it uses your Windows language (you can change it in Settings).
